How It Works

1. Choose Plan

Select Featured, VIP, VIP+ or Signature, then choose 3, 7, 15 or 30 days.

2. Submit Profile

Add your name, phone, WhatsApp, location, photos, services and at least one short video.

3. Pay & Go Live

Complete payment through M-Pesa. After approval, your profile appears on the site.
